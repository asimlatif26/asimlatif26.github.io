Projects & research

Key work

FairPl.ai: sports video intelligence for futsal venues

Live player and ball tracking for indoor futsal, running on a wall-mounted wide-angle camera. In the clip, every player is detected, given an ID that stays with them as they move, and sorted into a team automatically. The ball is tracked too, with a line linking it to the player in possession. Each camera is calibrated with a 14-coefficient rational distortion model and solvePnP with Levenberg–Marquardt refinement, so the views can be stitched into one panorama of the court for match streaming. Faces in the footage are blurred for privacy.

  1. Player detectionFind every player in each frame
  2. ID trackingKeep each player's ID as they move
  3. Team assignmentSort players into their teams
  4. Ball & possessionTrack the ball, link it to its player

Deep-learning pipeline for prognosis of Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia

An end-to-end pipeline that takes raw cell images and returns a diagnosis. Each stage filters or labels the cells before the next one sees them, so the final classifier only works on viable, correctly located white blood cells. Built on the team's own collected dataset, with a view to fitting into clinical workflows.

  1. Viable image detectionCNN rejects unusable frames
  2. Cell detectionLocate individual cells
  3. WBC classificationSort white blood cell types
  4. Subtype diagnosisLymphocytes vs. blast cells
